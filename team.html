Lab Directors

Dr. Qianhong Wu

Lab Director(Theory), Professor and Chair of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Villanova University, and Founding Director of the Cellular Biomechanics and Sports Science Laboratory.

Dr. Wu earned his PhD in Mechanical and Biomedical Engineering in 2005 from the City College of the City University of New York and New York Center for Biomedical Engineering, working with CUNY distinguished Professor Dr. Sheldon Weinbaum. He earned his MS in Electrical Engineering from the Institute of Electrical Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and his BS in Power Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

​

In August 2005, Dr. Wu started his faculty career as an assistant professor at Villanova University. He was promoted to associate professor with tenure in May 2011. Dr. Wu applies fundamental principles of fluid dynamics to bio and bio-inspired applications and has published extensively on these topics, which have been reported in Physical Review Letters, Science, Nature, Scientific American, PhysicsWeb and hundreds of public media around the world.

Dr. Rungun Nathan

Lab Director(Experiment), Professor and Program Chair of the Mechanical Engineering, and Director of the Berks Learning Factory at Penn State Berks.

Dr. Rungun Nathan earned his PhD in Mechanical Engineering from Drexel University, after completing his MS in Mechanical Engineering at the Louisiana State University, a post graduate diploma in Electronic Design Technology from Indian Institute of Science, and his BS in Mechanical Engineering from Mysore University, India. He began his faculty career at Villanova university in 1999 and then continued at Penn State Berks in 2007, where he is now Professor of Engineering, Program Chair of Mechanical Engineering, and Director of the Berks Learning Factory. His research interests span unmanned vehicles, mechatronics, bio-inspired applications, and engineering education.  

​​

Dr. Nathan has collaborated closely with colleagues at Villanova University, including Dr. Qianhong Wu on projects related to lift in porous media and Dr. Sergey Nersesov on control of RTAC systems, resulting in multiple graduate theses and journal publications. He is also active in ASEE and ASME, serving as a reviewer for leading engineering education and mechanics conferences and journals.​
