Cardiovascular Biomechanics

From transient arterial flow to quantitative measures of vascular function.

Flow-Mediated Dilation (FMD)

How can the response of an artery during flow-mediated dilation reveal its mechanical function?

Approach. We combine physics-based models and experimental measurements of transient blood flow, wall shear stress and arterial deformation. Fluid–structure interaction and endothelial mechanotransduction are treated as a coupled process.

Research significance. The aim is to develop quantitative vascular-health metrics grounded in the mechanics of the full FMD response.

Brain Biomechanics

Two connected branches examine the mechanics of traumatic brain injury and the accuracy of deep brain stimulation.

Traumatic Brain Injury

Biomimetic egg-yolk model

How can a soft body deform inside an intact rigid enclosure?

Approach. Controlled translational and rotational forcing of eggs isolates the role of surrounding fluid and rapid deceleration.

Research significance. This simplified system helps identify mechanisms to investigate in brain injury, while retaining the distinction between eggs and brains.

Representative publications

  • How to deform an egg yolk? On the study of soft matter deformation in a liquid environmentPhysics of Fluids · 2021

Smart Brain head surrogate

The skull makes direct observation of brain motion during impact difficult.

Approach. An instrumented biomimetic head surrogate measures compliant-brain motion, deformation and cerebrospinal-fluid pressurization under controlled loading.

Research significance. Repeatable experiments provide a physical basis for evaluating impact mechanisms and testing computational models.

Representative publications

  • Fluid mechanical study of rotation-induced traumatic brain injuryPhysical Review Fluids · 2025
  • Cavitation causes brain injuryPhysics of Fluids · 2021

CSF and arachnoid trabeculae mechanics

How do fluid and fibrous structures transmit short-duration loads between skull and brain?

Approach. Analytical, numerical and experimental studies examine transient flow through the porous subarachnoid space under translational and rotational loading.

Research significance. Understanding these interactions can inform the study of injury mechanisms and head protection.

Representative publications

  • Subarachnoid trabeculae suppress cerebrospinal fluid pressurization during translational impact loading using instrumented biomimetic head surrogatesActa Biomaterialia · 2026
  • Modeling of the brain movement and cerebrospinal fluid flow within porous subarachnoid space under translational impactsPhysics of Fluids · 2024
  • Modeling of periodical shearing flow in a fibrous space with applications in shear-induced brain injuryPhysics of Fluids · 2024

Imaging and clinical translation

Mechanical predictions need to be connected with observable changes in brain structure.

Approach. MRI processing and diffusion imaging are used alongside biomechanical investigation to study structural changes and their associations with clinical findings.

Research significance. This work seeks stronger connections between mechanics, imaging evidence and the assessment of traumatic brain injury.

Deep Brain Stimulation

Mechanics of electrode placement

  1. Clinical placement problem
  2. Mechanical interfaces
  3. Experimental characterization
  4. Computational modeling
  5. Patient-specific prediction

Accurate electrode placement is central to DBS, yet tissue heterogeneity and mechanical interfaces can influence the insertion trajectory.

Approach. We investigate electrode deviation through experimental characterization and computational models of tissue–probe interaction. Patient-specific brain mechanics provide the foundation for predictive modeling and digital-twin research.

Research significance. The goal is to understand the mechanical sources of deviation and support more reliable, patient-specific prediction of electrode trajectories.

Transport in Biological Systems

Fluid pathways connect tissue mechanics with biological signaling.

Skeletal muscle transport and muscle–bone interaction

How do interstitial flow and tissue structure influence signaling between muscle and bone?

Approach. Theory and experiments examine skeletal-muscle permeability and fluid flow during compression, linking tissue mechanics to transport pathways.

Research significance. Quantifying these constraints supports the study of biological signaling and muscle–bone interaction.
