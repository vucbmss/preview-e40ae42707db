Research

Our major research efforts are to apply fundamental principles of fluid dynamics to biomechanics, biomimetics, soft matter and fluid-assisted manufacturing. This research program is quite unique and was developed during the process of answering the following three challenging questions:

1. What are the “big questions” in my research area and how can I make contributions to the solution of them?

2. How can I build a distinguished research program with national and international reputations?

3. How can I successfully develop a sustained research program in terms of publications, funding and recognitions?

With a solid background in fundamental fluid mechanics, and with critical physical insights on various research problems, I ambitiously expand my research field to new areas that are interesting and promising. Especially, my training in both biomedical and mechanical engineering allows me to view the “big problems” from both bio- and bio-mimetic point of view. My research philosophy is to apply fundamental theory to cutting edge research areas, to uncover scientific mysteries, and to make an impact to the society.
